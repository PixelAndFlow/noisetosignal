# Roadmap

NoiseToSignal lets YouTube subscribers filter their viewing experience
down to just the creators and time window they choose — no algorithm,
no recommendations you didn't ask for.

## Phase 1 (current)

- **Signal mode** — filter your subscriptions by creator and timeframe
- **YouTube mode** — browse YouTube normally, side by side, so you can
  see the difference for yourself
- Subscription import and sync, watched-video tracking, keyboard
  shortcuts, installable as a PWA

## Known in-progress work

- Fixing a bug where older videos don't always show up in the feed
- Looking at ways around YouTube's subscription API limits for people
  who follow a large number of channels

## Later (not committed)

- Creator groups / saved filter sets
- Resume playback
- Native mobile app

## Notes

This roadmap is intentionally written in plain, outcome-focused
language — no API names, no internal architecture, no exact
thresholds. It's updated alongside build-in-public posts, not as a
substitute for them.
